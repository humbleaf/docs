## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

- **Account Information:** Email address, username, and authentication credentials
- **API Credentials:** API keys, access tokens, and related authentication data
- **Wallet Addresses:** Blockchain wallet addresses you connect to the Services
- **Communications:** Information you provide when contacting us for support or feedback
- **Payment Information:** Billing information as processed by our third-party payment providers

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage Data:** API call metadata, request/response timestamps, endpoints accessed, HTTP status codes
- **Task Telemetry:** Compute task metadata including task type, model identifiers, execution duration, token counts. We may store task inputs and outputs to provide and improve the Services
- **Agent Interaction Logs:** Metadata about agent deployment and agent-to-agent interactions
- **Device Information:** IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and referring URLs
- **Cookies:** Session management, authentication, and analytics (see Section 6)

### 1.3 Information from Blockchain Networks

Blockchain transactions, smart contract interactions, and wallet addresses are publicly available on blockchain networks. This data is not controlled by us and cannot be deleted or modified.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

- **Service Providers:** Cloud infrastructure, payment processing, analytics, and monitoring providers under contractual obligations
